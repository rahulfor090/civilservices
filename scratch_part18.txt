PART 1 — PRODUCT BRIEF (the target)
Build an exam-preparation education platform for a large competitive-exam coaching brand. The reference product serves millions of aspirants preparing for a national civil-services examination and a family of state-level examinations. It has three distinct surfaces that must be built as one coherent product.
1.1 The three surfaces
Surface	Primary user	Purpose
A. Public content portal	Anonymous visitor, search-engine crawler	Publish a very high volume of daily study content; capture leads; rank in search; drive traffic to the app/LMS and to paid programmes
B. Student LMS / app	Registered & paid student	Sell and deliver courses, test series and books; deliver video classes; run timed tests; show performance analytics
C. Admin back-office	Editorial staff, faculty, sales counsellors, finance, super-admin	Author and publish all content in two languages; build quizzes and tests; manage users, orders, leads, centres, exams, reports, and site configuration
A fourth surface, D. Marketing/lead-gen funnels (counselling booking, slot booking, webinar registration, open-mock-test registration, careers), is spread across A and C.
